# Privacy Policy

**Favorite Dua**  
**Effective date:** September 21, 2026

Favorite Dua is a personal Dua, image, audio, document, link, and scheduling application. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information the app handles

The app may handle content that you choose to create, import, record, or share with it, including:

- Dua text, translations, transliterations, references, notes, tags, and categories
- Images captured with the camera or selected from the device, including cropped images
- Audio recorded with the microphone or selected from the device
- Documents, videos, website links, and YouTube links
- Favorite selections, bundles, playback repeat counts, and schedule settings

This content is stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload your personal Dua collection to a developer-operated server.

## Device permissions

The app may request:

- **Camera and photos/files:** to capture, select, crop, import, preview, save, and share supported content
- **Microphone:** to record voice audio that you choose to attach to a Dua
- **Notifications and alarms:** to show scheduled reminders and run requested Dua playback
- **Media/audio access:** to select or play audio and other attachments

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Features that need a denied permission may not work.

## Sharing and external services

The app can receive supported content shared from other apps and can share content to apps that you select. When you open a website or YouTube link, content may be handled by an embedded web view, browser, YouTube, or another installed app. Those third parties process information under their own privacy policies.

The app includes Google Mobile Ads components. If advertising is enabled in a distributed build, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, diagnostics, approximate location derived from IP address, and interaction data to provide, measure, and protect ads. Google’s policies apply to that processing. Purchasing or enabling an ad-free entitlement disables ads where that feature is available.

## Data retention and deletion

Your collection remains on the device until you edit or delete it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Deleting a Dua is intended to remove its locally stored record and managed attachments. Content shared to another app is controlled by that app after sharing.

## Security and backups

The app uses Android application storage for managed content. You are responsible for keeping a secure backup of important content. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children’s privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated online service. Parents or guardians should supervise device permissions, content, sharing, links, and any advertising.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
